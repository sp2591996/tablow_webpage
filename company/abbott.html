Company overview

About Abbott India Ltd

Abbott India is one of the largest pharmaceutical companies in India by sales. It makes and markets Amoxyclav, and markets the Mixtard human insulin manufactured by Novo Nordisk. Thyronorm is its levothyroxine range. Abbott India Ltd was founded in 1910 and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Its parent company is Abbott Laboratories, Illinois (USA). It is listed on the stock exchange (NSE: ABBOTINDIA). Its main therapy areas are anti-infectives, diabetes, thyroid, gastroenterology, women's health.
