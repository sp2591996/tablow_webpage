Company overview

About GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals Ltd

GSK Pharmaceuticals is the Indian arm of UK-based GSK plc. Its India portfolio is led by anti-infectives such as Augmentin, alongside vaccines, dermatology and respiratory brands. GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals Ltd was founded in 1924 and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Its parent company is GSK plc, London (UK). It is listed on the stock exchange (NSE: GLAXO). Its main therapy areas are anti-infectives, vaccines, dermatology, respiratory.
