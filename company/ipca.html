Company overview

About Ipca Laboratories Ltd

Ipca Laboratories is a Mumbai-headquartered company and a leading maker of pain-relief and anti-malarial medicines, with a large export business. Pacimol is its paracetamol range. Ipca Laboratories Ltd was founded in 1949 and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. It is listed on the stock exchange (NSE: IPCALAB). Its main therapy areas are pain, anti-malarials, cardiology, dermatology.
