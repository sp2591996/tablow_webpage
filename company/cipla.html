Company overview

About Cipla Ltd

Cipla, founded by Dr K. A. Hamied in 1935, is one of India's largest pharmaceutical companies and a leader in inhaled respiratory medicines, including Foracort. Cipla Ltd was founded in 1935 by Dr Khwaja Abdul Hamied and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. It is listed on the stock exchange (NSE: CIPLA). Its main therapy areas are respiratory, anti-infectives, urology, hiv.

What does Cipla make?

Cipla focuses on 4 main therapy areas.

Respiratory

Inhalers and medicines for asthma, COPD and allergies.

Anti-infectives

Antibiotics and antimicrobials for bacterial and other infections.

Urology

Medicines for kidney, bladder and prostate conditions.

HIV

Where are Cipla medicines made?

Cipla runs plants in India and abroad, many approved by regulators such as the US FDA and WHO, and is one of the world's largest makers of inhalers.

Indian quality standardsEvery medicine sold in India must meet Indian Pharmacopoeia standards and be made under Schedule M good manufacturing practice.
