Company overview

About Macleods Pharmaceuticals Ltd

Macleods Pharmaceuticals is a privately held Mumbai-based company with a large presence in thyroid, heart, anti-TB and anti-infective medicines. Thyrox is its levothyroxine range. Macleods Pharmaceuticals Ltd was founded in 1989 and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. It is a privately held company. Its main therapy areas are thyroid, anti-tb, cardiology, anti-infectives.
