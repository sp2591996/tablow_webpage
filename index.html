FAQs

Questions about substitutes

What is a substitute medicine?

A substitute is another brand that contains the same salt (active ingredient), in the same strength and dosage form, as the medicine your doctor prescribed. It is often much cheaper.

Are substitutes as effective as the original brand?

Brands with the same salt, strength, form and release type contain the same active ingredient and must meet the same Indian Pharmacopoeia and GMP standards. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Can I still order the original brand?

Tablow's goal is to lower your medicine bill, so for each brand it recommends one same-salt alternative. If your doctor has asked you not to substitute, you can still order the original brand from its page.

Do I need a prescription to order?

No need to have one ready. Place your order and a Tablow doctor calls you to confirm the switch and issue a prescription. If you already have one, you can upload it.

How does Tablow choose the substitute?

We match salt, strength, dosage form and release type exactly, then pick an established Indian manufacturer with a lower price per unit.

Is a cheaper medicine lower quality?

No. Price differences mostly come from marketing and brand history. All brands sold in India must meet the same quality standards.
