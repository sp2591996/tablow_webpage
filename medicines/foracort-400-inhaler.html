What Indian rules say

  • Licensing: every medicine maker needs a manufacturing licence under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, and its products are tested against Indian Pharmacopoeia monographs.
  • Prescribing: the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations, 2002 ask doctors to prescribe drugs by generic name as far as possible.
  • Pricing: the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) sets ceiling prices for medicines on the national essential list, but brands can still be priced very differently below or outside those caps.

About Foracort 400 Inhaler

Uses, dosage, side effects and safety. This applies to Budamate 400 too, as both contain Budesonide 400 mcg + Formoterol 6 mcg per puff.

Overview

What is Foracort 400 Inhaler?

Foracort 400 Inhaler is a prescription medicine made by Cipla Ltd that contains Budesonide 400 mcg + Formoterol 6 mcg per puff. Budesonide + formoterol is a preventer inhaler for asthma and COPD. Budesonide is a low-dose steroid that calms swelling in the airways, and formoterol is a long-acting bronchodilator that relaxes the airway muscles within minutes. Used every day, it reduces attacks, night-time symptoms and the need for a reliever. It is a widely used inhaler combination for asthma and COPD. Combining a preventer and a long-acting reliever in one device makes daily treatment simpler.

Directions

How to take Foracort 400

How to take Foracort 400

  1. Shake the inhaler and remove the cap.
  2. Breathe out fully, away from the mouthpiece.
  3. Seal your lips around it, press once while breathing in slowly and deeply, then hold your breath for about 10 seconds.
  4. Wait 30 seconds before a second puff.
  5. Rinse your mouth with water and spit afterwards.

How it works

Budesonide reduces inflammation, swelling and mucus in the airways over days to weeks. Formoterol relaxes the muscles around the airways in 1–3 minutes, and the effect lasts about 12 hours. The two together control both the swelling and the tightening that cause asthma symptoms. Budamate 400 contains the same salt, so it works in exactly the same way.

Side effects

Side effects of Foracort 400 Inhaler

Common side effects

  • Hoarse voice
  • Sore throat
  • Oral thrush (white patches in the mouth)
  • Headache
  • Tremor
  • Palpitations

Usually mild. They often settle as your body adjusts.

Stop and get medical help if you notice

  • Breathing gets worse straight after a puff (paradoxical bronchospasm)
  • Fast or irregular heartbeat
  • Swelling of the face or throat (allergic reaction)

How to manage common side effects

Hoarse voice or sore throatRinse and gargle after each use, and use a spacer.
Oral thrushRinse after every dose. Tell your doctor about white patches; an antifungal gel may be needed.
Tremor or palpitationsUsually mild and short-lived. Tell your doctor if they are strong or persistent.

Precautions

Warnings and who should not take Foracort 400

Warnings and precautions

  • It is not for sudden severe attacks unless your doctor has set up a maintenance-and-reliever plan.
  • Seek urgent help if you need more puffs than usual or your reliever is not working.
  • Long-term high doses of inhaled steroids can affect bones and eyes; regular review is needed.
  • Do not stop it suddenly.

Who should not take Foracort 400

  • You are allergic to budesonide, formoterol or lactose (some devices)
  • You need relief from a severe attack: get emergency help instead
Good to know

Tips, lifestyle and storage for Foracort 400

Tips for taking it

  • Rinse your mouth with water and spit after every use to prevent thrush.
  • Use a spacer if you find it hard to press and breathe in at the same time.
  • Check your technique with a pharmacist when you switch to a new inhaler brand.
  • Do not stop suddenly even when you feel well.
  • Watch the dose counter and reorder when about 20 doses remain.

Lifestyle advice

  • Avoid smoke, dust and other known triggers.
  • Get a yearly flu vaccine; ask about the pneumococcal vaccine.
  • Follow a written asthma action plan.
  • Exercise regularly; warm up before exercise.
StorageStore below 30°C. Do not puncture or burn the canister, even when empty. Keep out of reach of children.

Using Budesonide + Formoterol safely 8

Is budesonide + formoterol a steroid inhaler?

Yes. Budesonide is a low-dose inhaled steroid that acts mainly in the lungs. Very little reaches the rest of the body.

How quickly does it work?

Formoterol relaxes the airways within 1–3 minutes. The steroid's full benefit builds over 1–2 weeks of regular use.

Can children use it?

Some strengths are used in children, as a doctor prescribes. A spacer helps.

Do I still need a blue reliever inhaler?

That depends on your plan. Some doctors use this inhaler as both preventer and reliever; others prescribe a separate reliever.

How do I know when the inhaler is empty?

Check the dose counter. Reorder when about 20 doses remain.

Can I use it during an asthma attack?

Only if your doctor has prescribed it as a reliever too. In a severe attack, get emergency help.

Will it affect my growth or bones?

At usual doses the risk is low. Children's growth and adults' bone health are reviewed with long-term high doses.

Why should I use a spacer?

A spacer gets more medicine into your lungs and less into your mouth, which improves results and reduces side effects.
