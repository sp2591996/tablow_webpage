What Indian rules say

  • Licensing: every medicine maker needs a manufacturing licence under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, and its products are tested against Indian Pharmacopoeia monographs.
  • Prescribing: the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations, 2002 ask doctors to prescribe drugs by generic name as far as possible.
  • Pricing: the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) sets ceiling prices for medicines on the national essential list, but brands can still be priced very differently below or outside those caps.

About Dolo 650 Tablet

Uses, dosage, side effects and safety. This applies to Pacimol 650 too, as both contain Paracetamol 650 mg.

Overview

What is Dolo 650 Tablet?

Dolo 650 Tablet is a pain and fever medicine made by Micro Labs Ltd that contains Paracetamol 650 mg. Paracetamol is a pain reliever and fever reducer. It treats fever, headache, body ache, toothache and period pain, and is the usual first choice for fever in India, including in dengue, where ibuprofen and aspirin are avoided. At the right dose it is gentle on the stomach, but too much can seriously damage the liver. Paracetamol is one of the most widely used medicines in the world. The 650 mg strength is widely used by adults in India for fever and body ache.

Directions

How to take Dolo 650

How to take Dolo 650

  1. Take one tablet with a glass of water, with or without food.
  2. Leave at least 4–6 hours between doses.
  3. Do not take more than 4 g in 24 hours, counting every product that contains paracetamol.

How it works

Paracetamol works mainly in the brain and spinal cord, where it reduces prostaglandins, chemicals that signal pain and raise body temperature. It resets the brain's temperature centre to bring fever down. It has little anti-inflammatory effect, which is why it is gentle on the stomach. Pacimol 650 contains the same salt, so it works in exactly the same way.

If you miss a doseParacetamol is usually taken only when needed. If you are on a regular schedule, take the dose when you remember, keeping at least 4 hours from the last one.
If you take too muchA paracetamol overdose is a medical emergency even if you feel well. Go to a hospital at once; the antidote works best within 8 hours.
Side effects

Side effects of Dolo 650 Tablet

Common side effects

  • Nausea
  • Mild stomach upset
  • Skin rash (uncommon)
  • Allergic reaction (rare)

Usually mild. They often settle as your body adjusts.

Stop and get medical help if you notice

  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or pain in the upper right abdomen (liver damage)
  • Red, blistering or peeling skin (rare serious skin reaction)
  • Swelling of the face or throat, or difficulty breathing
  • Unusual bruising or bleeding (very rare)

Precautions

Warnings and who should not take Dolo 650

Warnings and precautions

  • Never take more than the maximum daily dose. Liver damage from an overdose may not cause symptoms for 1–2 days.
  • Many cold, flu and combination painkillers also contain paracetamol.
  • Fever lasting more than 3 days needs a doctor, especially in dengue or malaria season.
  • Keep out of reach of children; accidental overdose is dangerous.

Who should not take Dolo 650

  • You are allergic to paracetamol
  • You have severe liver disease
  • You already take another medicine containing paracetamol (many cold and flu remedies do)
  • You drink alcohol heavily every day, unless your doctor advises
Good to know

Tips, lifestyle and storage for Dolo 650

Tips for taking it

  • Leave at least 4 hours between doses.
  • Check cold, flu and combination painkillers for paracetamol before taking them together.
  • Drink plenty of fluids when you have a fever.
  • See a doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days or pain more than 5 days.
  • In dengue season, use paracetamol rather than ibuprofen or aspirin for fever, and see a doctor.

Lifestyle advice

  • Rest and drink plenty of fluids during a fever.
  • Wear light clothing; avoid heavy wrapping during fever.
  • For repeated headaches, look at sleep, screen time and water intake.
  • Avoid painkillers on more than 10–15 days a month without advice, as this can cause rebound headaches.
StorageStore below 30°C in a dry place. Keep out of reach of children.

Using Paracetamol safely 8

Is paracetamol safe to take every day?

At normal doses for short periods, yes. Daily use for weeks should be discussed with a doctor.

How long does paracetamol take to work?

Usually 30–60 minutes. The effect lasts 4–6 hours.

Can I take paracetamol on an empty stomach?

Yes. It is gentle on the stomach and can be taken with or without food.

Is paracetamol the same as acetaminophen?

Yes. Acetaminophen is the name used in the USA for the same medicine.

Can I take paracetamol for dengue fever?

Yes, it is preferred for fever in dengue. Avoid ibuprofen, aspirin and diclofenac, which raise bleeding risk.

What is the difference between paracetamol 500 mg and 650 mg?

A 650 mg tablet has 30% more paracetamol. Both are used in adults; follow the dose your doctor or the pack advises.

Can I take paracetamol with ibuprofen?

Doctors sometimes advise alternating them for a short time for high fever or pain. Ask your doctor or pharmacist first.

How many paracetamol 650 mg tablets can I take in a day?

Most adults should take no more than 4 g a day, which is 6 tablets of 650 mg, at least 4 hours apart. Your doctor may advise less.
