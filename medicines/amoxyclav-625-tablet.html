Product overview

What is Amoxyclav 625 Tablet?

Amoxyclav 625 Tablet is a prescription medicine by Abbott India Ltd containing Amoxicillin 500 mg + Clavulanic Acid 125 mg. It is a same-salt substitute for Augmentin 625 Duo Tablet and costs 37% less. Amoxicillin + clavulanic acid (co-amoxiclav) is a combination antibiotic. Amoxicillin kills bacteria, and clavulanic acid stops bacterial enzymes from breaking amoxicillin down. Together they treat infections that resist amoxicillin alone. It works only on bacterial infections, not on colds, flu or other viral infections. It belongs to the penicillin group of antibiotics and is widely prescribed in India for ear, nose, throat, chest, urinary, skin and dental infections. Doctors choose it when they suspect bacteria that make beta-lactamase, an enzyme that makes plain amoxicillin ineffective.

Directions

How to take Amoxyclav 625

How to take Amoxyclav 625

  1. Take this medicine in the dose and for the length of time your doctor advises.
  2. Swallow the tablet whole with a glass of water.
  3. do not chew or crush it.
  4. Take it at the start of a meal.
  5. Space the doses evenly, for example at 8 am and 8 pm.

How it works

Amoxicillin stops bacteria from building their cell walls, so they burst and die. Many bacteria defend themselves with an enzyme called beta-lactamase that destroys amoxicillin. Clavulanic acid blocks this enzyme, so amoxicillin keeps working against bacteria that would otherwise be resistant.

Side effects

Side effects of Amoxyclav 625 Tablet

Common side effects

  • Diarrhoea
  • Nausea
  • Vomiting
  • Skin rash
  • Vaginal yeast infection (thrush)

Usually mild. They often settle as your body adjusts.

Stop and get medical help if you notice

  • Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing (allergic reaction)
  • Severe, watery or bloody diarrhoea, which can happen weeks later (C. difficile colitis)
  • Yellow skin or eyes, or dark urine (liver injury)
  • Widespread blistering or peeling rash

How to manage common side effects

DiarrhoeaDrink plenty of water and oral rehydration solution. Do not take anti-diarrhoea medicine without asking your doctor. Contact a doctor for watery or bloody stools.
Nausea or vomitingTake the tablet at the start of a meal and avoid rich, spicy food. Eat small, frequent meals.
Vaginal thrushTell your doctor about itching or white discharge. A short antifungal treatment may be needed.

Precautions

Warnings and who should not take Amoxyclav 625

Warnings and precautions

  • Tell your doctor if you have ever had an allergic reaction to any antibiotic.
  • Long or repeated courses can lead to fungal infections or C. difficile diarrhoea.
  • It can make the combined contraceptive pill less effective if you have vomiting or diarrhoea. Use extra protection.
  • Some urine glucose tests can give false results during treatment.

Who should not take Amoxyclav 625

  • You are allergic to penicillin, amoxicillin or any beta-lactam antibiotic (such as cephalosporins)
  • You had jaundice or liver problems with amoxicillin + clavulanic acid before
  • You have glandular fever (infectious mononucleosis), as a rash is more likely
Good to know

Tips, lifestyle and storage

Tips for taking it

  • Antibiotics do not treat colds or flu. Use them only when a doctor prescribes them.
  • Take it at the start of a meal: this reduces nausea and helps clavulanic acid absorb.
  • Finish the whole course even if you feel better, to stop the infection coming back.
  • Curd or a probiotic may ease mild diarrhoea. Report severe or bloody diarrhoea straight away.
  • Never keep leftover tablets for a future infection.

Lifestyle advice

  • Rest and drink plenty of fluids while you recover.
  • Complete the full course even when you feel better.
  • Wash your hands often to avoid spreading infection.
  • Do not share antibiotics or use leftover tablets.
StorageStore below 25°C in the original pack to protect from moisture. Keep out of reach of children.

About Amoxyclav 625 and Augmentin 625 6

Is Amoxyclav 625 a good substitute for Augmentin 625 Duo?

Yes. It has the same salts (amoxicillin 500 mg + clavulanic acid 125 mg), the same strength and the same tablet form. It is made by Abbott India. After you order, a Tablow doctor calls you to confirm the switch and issue your prescription.

What is Amoxyclav 625 used for?

It treats bacterial infections such as sinusitis, tonsillitis, ear infections, bronchitis, pneumonia, urinary tract infections, skin infections and dental abscesses.

Who makes Amoxyclav 625?

Amoxyclav 625 is made by Abbott India Ltd, the Indian arm of US-based Abbott Laboratories.

Should Amoxyclav 625 be taken before or after food?

Take it at the start of a meal to reduce stomach upset and help absorption.

Can Amoxyclav 625 cause diarrhoea?

Yes, it is the most common side effect. Contact your doctor if it is severe, watery or bloody.

Do I need a prescription for Amoxyclav 625?

Using Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid safely 8

Can I stop the antibiotic when I feel better?

No. Stopping early can let the infection come back and makes bacteria more likely to become resistant. Finish the full course.

Does amoxicillin + clavulanic acid work for cold and flu?

No. Colds and flu are caused by viruses, and antibiotics only work on bacteria.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, when a doctor prescribes it. Children usually get the syrup, with the dose worked out from their body weight.

Can it cause an allergic reaction?

Yes. Stop it and get urgent help if you get swelling of the face or throat, breathing difficulty or a widespread rash.

Why is clavulanic acid added to amoxicillin?

Many bacteria make an enzyme that destroys amoxicillin. Clavulanic acid blocks that enzyme so amoxicillin can work.

Can I drink milk with it?

Yes. Milk does not affect it. Taking it with food is recommended.

Does it affect birth control pills?

It does not usually reduce their effect, but vomiting or diarrhoea can. Use a backup method if either happens.

What if I get diarrhoea after finishing the course?

Diarrhoea up to two months later can be a sign of C. difficile infection. See a doctor, especially if it is watery or bloody.
